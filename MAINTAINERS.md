## How additional maintainers are promoted

Per [`GOVERNANCE.md`](GOVERNANCE.md), additional maintainers are promoted based on sustained contribution. The current criteria are:

- ≥ 6 merged pull requests over ≥ 3 months
- At least one substantive code contribution (not docs-only)
- Demonstrated good judgement in code review discussions
- A clear contribution area (e.g., "small molecules", "documentation", "plugins") that the maintainer would own

A promotion is proposed by the BDFL or self-nominated; the BDFL has final say. New maintainers are added to this file and given merge rights in the same PR.

## How to step down

Maintainers may step down at any time by opening a PR removing themselves from this list, or by emailing the BDFL. There is no expectation of explanation.
